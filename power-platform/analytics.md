# Analytics

Does anyone use the **Tenant-level analytics** in the Power Platform Admin center?

Try to enable and monitor your tenant...

## How to enable Tenant-level analytics?

Firstly, ensure you have a least admin role as below:

* Power Platform admin
* Microsoft 365 Global admin
* Delegated admin

Then, sign in to the [**Power Platform admin center**](https://admin.powerplatform.microsoft.com/) select **Settings >** and select **Analytics** menu item

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/CleanShot 2024-12-29 at 10.20.50@2x.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Analytics feature</p></figcaption></figure>

Afterward, you **Enable** and click **Save** to turn on this monitoring feature.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/CleanShot 2024-12-29 at 10.24.56@2x.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Enables tenant-level analytics</p></figcaption></figure>

## After enabling the Tenant-level analytics?

Go to **Analytics** and select **Power Automate / Power Apps.** The insight data will appear in the **Overview** ta&#x62;**.**

{% hint style="success" %}
**The Power Platform** collects telemetry data about operations and user activities across environments and extensions. Through tenant-level analytics, admins can view and compare insights from this data across their managed environments. This gives tenant admins access to reports covering all environments within their tenant.
{% endhint %}
